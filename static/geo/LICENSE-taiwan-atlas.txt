static/geo/tw-counties.geo.json is converted (TopoJSON -> GeoJSON, coordinates rounded to 3 decimals,
properties reduced to name/code, and 台 in county names written as 臺)
from counties-10t.json of taiwan-atlas 2021.9.20 (https://github.com/dkaoster/taiwan-atlas),
which redistributes the Ministry of the Interior's 村里界圖(TWD97經緯度) (https://data.gov.tw/dataset/7438),
published under the 政府資料開放授權條款－第1版 (Open Government Data License, version 1.0).
